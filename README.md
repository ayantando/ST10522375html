# Luxe Bags

Luxe Bags is a fashion e-commerce website offering chic, luxury, and vintage-inspired bags for men and women. The site was built to give customers quality products alongside a simple, convenient online shopping experience.

## About

Luxe Bags was founded by Ayanda Msomi, an entrepreneur from Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa. What began as an idea in a notebook in 2022 was formally launched in 2024, gaining recognition on 28 July 2024 and expanding across South Africa before reaching neighboring countries such as Namibia and Zimbabwe. In 2025, the brand partnered with South African artist Sabelo Mzobe, boosting its visibility beyond the South African market.

The brand's motto, **"Be a Dreamer,"** reflects its founding belief that ideas can become reality when people are willing to dream and pursue their goals.

## Mission

To deliver fashionable, practical, and accessible bags for both men and women — combining chic, luxury, and vintage-inspired designs with an affordable, convenient online shopping experience that helps customers express their individuality.
